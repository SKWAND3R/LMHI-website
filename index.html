Liga Medicorum Homoeopathica Internationalis (LMHI)

It has always been an endeavour of LMHI to promote the best of homoeopathic practices across the globe through interaction and sharing of knowledge.

The international homeopathic medical society Liga Medicorum Homoeopathica Internationalis (LMHI), established in Rotterdam in 1925, represents homeopathic physicians in more than 70 countries worldwide. The purpose of the association is the development and safeguarding of homeopathy globally, and the creation of a link among licensed homeopaths, medical diploma holders, societies, and individuals interested in homeopathy.
